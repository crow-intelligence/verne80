This project is generated by AI tools with minimal human intervention. The basic idea comes from my son. We were reading Verne's book during this summer and constantly checked the stages of the journey on globe.

The named entities (in our case names of people and places) were extracted from the book with Gemini. The chapter summaries were generated with Gemini.After the data collection, Claude Code did the hard work of generating the visualization and the site based on our basic idea.
